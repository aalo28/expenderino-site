Common questions

How do I import my bank data?
Settings → Import / Export → Bank Statement, then choose a camt.053/.052, MT940, OFX/QFX, CSV, or PDF file exported from your e-banking. For other layouts, use Generic CSV Import.

Where is my data stored?
On your device, and — if you enable iCloud — in your own iCloud account. See our Privacy Policy.

A transaction imported twice.
Settings → Data → Merge Duplicate Transactions, or delete the extra row by swiping it.
